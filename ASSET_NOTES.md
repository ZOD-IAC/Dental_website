# Generated dental treatment assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool and optimized to WebP for the website. Real clinic and dentist photographs were preserved.

Shared prompt template:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: dental clinic treatment card illustration. Create one premium landscape 3D still life of [SUBJECT]. Warm ivory background, sage green accents, natural porcelain textures, soft studio shadows, subtle clinical precision, calm refined editorial art direction. Large central subject with breathing space suitable for a 3:2 card. No text, labels, logos, people, blood or watermark. Educational conceptual illustration, not a clinical outcome photograph.

- `public/image/generated/implant.webp`: a single dental implant model, white molar crown suspended above a titanium implant screw, soft gum base.
- `public/image/generated/whitening.webp`: a clean white tooth model beside a dental shade guide and a whitening tray.
- `public/image/generated/root-canal.webp`: a simplified molar cutaway model with visible root canals and a slim endodontic file, non-graphic.
- `public/image/generated/crowns.webp`: a white ceramic crown hovering above a prepared model tooth with adjacent natural tooth models.
- `public/image/generated/oral-surgery.webp`: a clean dental jaw model showing a wisdom tooth, beside a small dental mirror, no real surgery.
- `public/image/generated/aligners.webp`: a transparent clear aligner resting over a clean model of upper teeth.
